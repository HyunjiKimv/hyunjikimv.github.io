About

I completed my Ph.D. at the Sports Engineering Lab, Department of Kinesiology, Seoul National University (2021–2026), with a dissertation on deep learning–based VO2/VCO2 estimation and fitness assessment for individuals with spinal cord injury, aimed at making self-directed exercise monitoring accessible outside the lab. Before that, my M.S. work examined how shoe conditions shape the biomechanics of recreational runners. I originally trained in chemistry education (B.S., Seoul National University) before moving into human exercise science.

I'm now a Post-Doctoral Researcher at the Robotics Institute, Seoul National University, working on wearable sensors, sensory feedback, and assistive technology — and mentoring the next group of researchers coming up through the lab.

Wearable assistive technology Biomechanics of underrepresented populations Inclusive fitness monitoring Musculoskeletal modeling
